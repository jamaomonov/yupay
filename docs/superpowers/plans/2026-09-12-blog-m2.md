# Blog — M2: Storefront, sitemap, llms.txt, scheduled publish

> Implement task-by-task. Do not start leftover M3 (FAQ editor UX, event/pin
> polish). TipTap + `blog_image` already shipped on `feat/blog`.

**Goal:** A reader on `apps/web` can open `/{locale}/blog` and
`/{locale}/blog/{slug}`, follow a header link, see pinned/latest posts on
`/store/{brand}`, and reach a live buy card. Crawlers get sitemap URLs,
`/llms.txt` titles, and JSON-LD. Due `scheduled` rows become `published`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-12-blog-design.md` §11–15.

## Constraints

- No `loading.tsx` under `[locale]/blog` (prerender-guard, same as `/store`).
- `export const revalidate = 300` on article + index; sitemap/llms stay 3600.
- Never `next build` on the host. Tag public fetches `blog`.
- i18n: `web.nav.blog` + `web.blog.*` in ru/en/uz in the same change.
- Buy card reads `catalog` live. Do not invent a second product tile.
- Index stays ISR: brand hub is the store block, not `?brand=` (query would
  dynamize `/blog`).
- Mutations stay admin-only. Public GETs remain keyless.

## Tasks

1. Public DTO: list/detail carry `id`; detail carries `locale_slugs` for
   hreflang. Scheduler job calls `admin_service.publish_post`.
2. `apps/web` lib + cards + `/blog` pages + header/footer + brand block.
3. Sitemap (per-translation URL, `lastmod = updated_at` day) + `/llms.txt`
   «Гайды и новости» (~20 ru).
4. Docs: product flow, read sequence, module-map, ADR-0072 M2 note, OpenAPI.

## Out of scope

- Mini App, FAQ editor, comments, `blog.yupay.uz`.
